A real train possibility

Description
In “Explore other ways,” reveal actual Sallins–Heuston trains for the offer date. The 08:52 train is scheduled to reach Heuston at 09:26. That leaves 64 minutes before Aoife's reported 10:30 appointment; let her assess onward travel and a buffer before calling it workable.
Axis
Discover a workable travel alternative.
Basis
External: the official NTA Irish Rail GTFS downloaded successfully and was parsed using service calendars, date exceptions and stop sequence for 8 October. Pickup at Sallins and dropoff at Heuston are permitted. Direct: the PRD's “Explore other ways” slot and reported deadline. Reasoned: showing one relevant service avoids requiring a general journey planner.
Rationale
It turns “maybe I could take the train” into a specific option with real times. A bundled dated card makes the same moment work without network access during the demo.
Downsides
The timetable covers stations only. Home-to-station access, onward travel, fare and personal suitability remain unresolved. No fare files were present in the operator ZIP. Scheduled arrival is not a guarantee or spare-capacity claim. Selecting a train neither reserves a reward nor proves the vehicle stayed out of the zone.
Confidence
95% in the verified schedule; whole-journey feasibility remains unconfirmed.
Complexity
Medium: a narrow service selector and connecting-leg inputs. The prepared data already exists; app integration does not.

2 · Medium effort · Conditional on data preparation

Explore a measured morning

Description
Keep the measured N7 morning profile fixed while Aoife moves her crossing marker from 08:15 to 09:15. Let her compare observed volume around the two windows and inspect the underlying dates.
Axis
Recognise the actual corridor and road history.
Basis
Direct: this is the PRD's core Screen 2 interaction. External: TII documents public traffic downloads; an old per-vehicle CSV was tested and contains timestamps, lane, class and speed fields. An appropriate dashboard export is another route to investigate.
Rationale
It connects her familiar morning to actual road observations. The same prepared profile can inform the council-planner screen.
Downsides
Recent CSV probes returned HTTP 403. No complete direction-resolved N7 profile has been prepared. The tested class aggregate is a daily total; the minutes aggregate is daily coverage. Neither supplies a time-binned curve. Lane direction and completeness need validation. Historic counts must show actual dates and cannot become a forecast, faster-journey claim or arrival estimate.
Confidence
90% in the product fit; 60% in obtaining suitable data promptly. These are research judgments, not measured probabilities.
Complexity
Medium, with uncertain access effort. Prefer a suitable export; otherwise preprocess timestamped observations. Retain the labelled sample chart until real evidence is ready.
3 · Low–medium effort · Geography verified

Recognise the corridor

Description
Use recognisable Naas/N7 geography, a direction arrow and a real counter marker in the offer and “Find your flex” views. Keep the agreement's bounded section visible as Aoife proceeds to review.
Axis
Recognise the actual corridor and road history.
Basis
External: the TII location GeoJSON downloaded successfully. It lists candidate N7 sites Kill/Johnstown (20071) and Kingswood/Citywest (1071). Attributed OpenStreetMap geometry can provide recognisable roads. Direct: the PRD requires a consistent section, direction and counter reference.
Rationale
Real places help the offer feel local and understandable, with less data preparation than the road chart.
Downsides
The counter file is historical metadata. A sensor point does not define a FlexZone, prove current coverage or establish the relevant direction. Preserve the explicitly schematic Entry A–Exit B agreement until a real section is selected. OSM geometry has attribution requirements; do not prefetch public OSM tiles for an offline demo.
Confidence
90% in available geography; agreement boundaries and current coverage remain unconfirmed.
Complexity
Low–medium for a bounded local geometry view; an interactive full map adds unnecessary integration work for this story.

Recommendation: start with the verified train option and recognisable geography. Add genuine traffic history when a suitable export is obtained. Keep the PRD's later-crossing path as the primary fixture; a train becomes an additional feasible option only after its connecting legs are supported. Bundle evidence for the fixed demo date.
