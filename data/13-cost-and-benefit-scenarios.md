# N7 cost and benefit scenario inputs

**Data status: calculations from a real denominator with assumed intervention behaviour.** Packaged 4 October 2026 from existing research; no fresh online collection.

Denominator: 1 October morning CAR 12,426 and Any 17,821. Assumptions: unchanged other vehicle classes; one additional changed morning arrival per relevant participant; EUR 3 total per day; 80% additionality; full completion in the reward budget. Distance column assumes each change eliminates a whole 50 km daily round trip, before replacement transport and rebound. It is zero for pure retiming.

Separate synthetic capacity model: hourly arrivals 4,100 / 4,400 / 4,300 / 4,000 from 06:00-10:00; hypothetical capacity 4,000 vehicles/hour; target 3,800/hour. Removal profile 300 / 600 / 500 / 200 = 1,600. This is not measured N7 demand or capacity. At 80% additionality, 2,000 rewarded person-days cost EUR 6,000/day or EUR 300,000 over 50 weekdays, excluding delivery.

Potential benefit inputs: measured person-hours, net vehicle operating costs, net CO2e, safety appraisal, maintenance changes and actual deferred capital. No causal savings supplied. Illustrative unverified carbon factor 0.18 kg CO2e/km and 50 km/day may be used only as explicit assumption sliders, not an official N7 emissions factor. The full [savings framework](16-benefit-areas-and-scenarios.md) distinguishes societal benefits from Exchequer cash. Avoid double counting and account for participant inconvenience, displaced queues, replacement transport and programme resource costs.
