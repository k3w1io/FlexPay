## What is real

One public camera, two verified views; one counter identifier and displayed coordinate; five dates of reported morning counts; 480 five-minute observations; 40 derived class/hour records; ten class/day summary records; source and access evidence.

## What is fictional or missing

Twelve participant profiles and deliberately invalid plate tokens, sixty individual commute records, 24 observation-result fixtures, twelve decision fixtures, wallet and appeal stories, offer terms, performance values and planning scenarios. No real ANPR matching, net traffic improvement, journey-time series, weather, collision history, approved payment integration or calibrated N7 capacity is supplied.

Use the data-status label on every dashboard card and chart. Historical measured counts and fictional outcome charts must be visibly distinguishable. Real counts describe passing vehicles; synthetic profiles do not identify those vehicles. Reward assumption: EUR 3 total per participant/day covering outward and return journeys. All files in this folder are simple Markdown with tables or fenced JSON for reuse.
